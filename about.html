The Common Places

Helping curious people decide what deserves their attention.

01 / PURPOSE

Why We Exist

We live in an age of extraordinary abundance.

Books arrive faster than we can read them. Articles accumulate in unread folders. Podcasts queue endlessly. AI can generate summaries of almost anything within seconds.

Yet many thoughtful people feel less certain about what truly deserves their attention.

The Common Places exists to answer that question.

We believe the modern challenge is not access to knowledge. It is judgment.

02 / PRINCIPLES

What We Believe

Depth over speed

Some ideas deserve an entire weekend. Some deserve ten minutes. Wisdom begins with knowing the difference.

Curation over aggregation

We are not trying to collect everything worth reading. We are trying to identify what matters most.

Understanding over summaries

A summary can convey a conclusion. It cannot always convey the reasoning, the tension, the stories, or the gradual construction of an idea. Some works must be experienced, not compressed.

Timeless ideas over trending content

We care more about what remains useful in five years than what is popular for five days.

Editorial judgment over algorithms

Technology can help discover resources. It cannot replace thoughtful selection, context, and perspective.

03 / FORMAT

What We Publish

Everything revolves around Issues.

An Issue is not a blog post. It is not a newsletter.

An Issue is a carefully designed learning journey built around one important question.

Each Issue combines books, essays, articles, podcasts, lectures, research papers, and courses into a coherent editorial experience.

We never reproduce copyrighted work. We guide readers toward original creators and add our own commentary, context, and connections.

The Common Places

A publication about judgment, curation, and attention — one question per issue, argued properly.
