Why we exist

Judgment, not consumption

We're not trying to help you consume more. We're trying to help you spend your attention as though it were what it has always actually been — the scarcest resource you have.

How we work

We show our reasoning

We make considered choices about what's worth lingering over, and just as importantly, we show our working — including what we chose to leave out, and why.

How often

Slow, on purpose

One question per issue, followed through its backstory, its complications, and how it plays out in everyday life — rather than a list of links dressed up as insight.
