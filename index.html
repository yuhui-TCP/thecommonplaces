If a quiet person is simply listening, thinking or content, why do we keep asking what’s wrong — and who is the question really for?

The Quiet One — why “you’re so quiet today” is a question about the room, and what it asks of the person who hears it

For people who have been asked why they’re so quiet and answered with a joke — and for those who asked, kindly, so the table could get going again. We begin with a question — about silence, rooms, and who a question is really for — then follow the thread through its backstory, its complications, the ideas it opens up, and how they take shape in the real world.

Why we exist

Judgment, not consumption

We're not trying to help you consume more. We're trying to help you spend your attention as though it were what it has always actually been — the scarcest resource you have.

How we work

We show our reasoning

We make considered choices about what's worth lingering over, and just as importantly, we show our working — including what we chose to leave out, and why.

How often

Slow, on purpose

One question per issue, followed through its backstory, its complications, and how it plays out in everyday life — rather than a list of links dressed up as insight.
